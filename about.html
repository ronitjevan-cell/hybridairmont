About Us

Autobiography

A Long Publishing History

Founded in 1957, Airmont Publishing started its journey with a mission to bring timeless literature to the masses. Over the decades, we have published hundreds of award-winning works, establishing ourselves as a trusted partner for authors across all genres.

Publishing Options

Traditional and Hybrid

At Airmont Publishing, we offer both traditional and hybrid publishing options. While traditional remains a selective process, we understand that every story deserves a chance to be told. Our hybrid route allows writers more creative control.

Through our global distribution network, your books will be available on platforms like Amazon, Apple Books, and Barnes & Noble.
